# V4's soundtrack is the clock

Éric Lemoine's voice, and the moment at which every picture, Spread and Archive film appears, are taken from version V4 of the Film: its soundtrack is the Journey's clock and its picture is the source of the cues. The Dossier also holds voice-only recordings (`Narration_VF3_P1…P5`) and a pptx with a timing on every slide, which look like the cleaner sources, but both belong to an older take: the wording differs from V4 ("une semaine et 6 missions" against V4's "10 jours et 11 missions"), the pauses do not line up, and their combined length matches V3, not V4. The pptx is still used for what it does hold: the original, sharper files of the pictures.

## Consequences

- The Narration cannot be re-edited or cleaned phrase by phrase; whatever is under the voice in V4 stays under it.
- A Chapter opening is not part of V4. The site holds the soundtrack while it plays, so the Journey runs slightly longer than the Film and the Timeline measures the Narration only.
- Captions follow what Éric Lemoine says in V4, even where his written script or a document says otherwise.
