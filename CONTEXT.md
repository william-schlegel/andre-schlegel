# André Schlegel — Le Parcours

A website that tells the military and aeronautical career of Commandant André Schlegel (1935–1962), as narrated by his grandson Éric Lemoine.

## Language

**Dossier**:
The family archive gathered by Éric Lemoine to make the Film: the Carnets de vol, personal photographs, citations, official documents, and the pictures and films he collected from archives and the internet.
_Avoid_: source folder, assets, media library

**Bonus**:
A document or picture from the Dossier that Éric Lemoine did not show in the Film, offered to the visitor as an optional extra alongside a Chapter. It never replaces or interrupts what the Film shows.
_Avoid_: extra, supplement, annex

**Film**:
The 55-minute video made by Éric Lemoine from his presentation. Version V4 is the reference; V3 and V5 are not used.
_Avoid_: video, presentation, V5

**Narration**:
Éric Lemoine's spoken account of André Schlegel's career, from his 1935 diploma to his death in 1962, as heard in the Film.
_Avoid_: voice-over, commentary, transcript

**Journey**:
The visitor's guided passage through the whole Narration, from Prologue to Epilogue.
_Avoid_: tour (clashes with Tours and with "tour d'opération"), slideshow, presentation

**Chapter**:
One unbroken stretch of the Narration, named after the place or episode it covers. The Journey has nine: Tours, Algérie, Campagne de France, Algérie sous Vichy, L'école anglaise, Elvington, Une nuit sur l'Allemagne, Les missions, Après la guerre.
_Avoid_: section, part, period (a place visited twice, like Algeria, is two Chapters)

**Introduction**:
What the visitor finds before the Journey starts and before any Narration: a lamp-lit table with the closed Carnet of 1938, André Schlegel's portrait and his decorations. Opening the Carnet begins the Journey.
_Avoid_: landing page, home, splash screen, Ouverture (which is spoken)

**Ouverture**:
The first moments of the Narration, before the first Chapter, in which Éric Lemoine introduces himself and his sources.
_Avoid_: introduction, preamble

**Chapter opening**:
The short cut scene that begins each Chapter while the Narration waits: the passage from the previous Chapter's place and time to the new one, over period music that fades as Éric Lemoine speaks again. It shows only what is attested: the Route on the Chart, the new Posting, and the change of light.
_Avoid_: transition, interlude, intro (the Introduction is something else)

**Posting**:
One assignment of André Schlegel to a base and a unit, with his rank and its dates, such as "Lieutenant, Base de Biskra, 19e Escadre, 4 septembre 1939 – 12 avril 1940". In French, an affectation.
_Avoid_: assignment, station (a Station is an RAF base, or a crew position in the Halifax)

**Scene**:
What the visitor sees and can touch during one Chapter. Every Chapter has its own Scene.
_Avoid_: slide, page, screen

**Timeline**:
The whole Narration laid out from start to end, divided into Chapters and labelled with the story's own dates and events. The visitor moves along it to reach any moment of the Journey.
_Avoid_: progress bar, scrubber, chronology, frieze (it is not a calendar drawn to scale)

**Chart**:
The single map of the whole Journey, drawn in the manner of a period aeronautical chart, with the place names and borders of the time.
_Avoid_: map (when it could mean a modern web map), carte interactive, plan

**Route**:
The line André Schlegel's career traces on the Chart, from posting to posting and from base to target, growing as the Narration advances.
_Avoid_: path, trajectory, itinerary

**Recognition model**:
A solid, single-colour scale model of one of the aircraft in the story, shaped like the models wartime aircrews used to learn aircraft by silhouette. It carries no paint or markings, and the visitor can turn it in their hands.
_Avoid_: 3D plane, aircraft model, maquette peinte

**Close-up**:
The visitor's deliberate examination of one object — a document, the Carnet de vol, a plane — during which the Narration waits and then resumes where it stopped.
_Avoid_: modal, popup, lightbox, zoom

**Prologue**:
The Chapter "Tours" (1936–1939), before the war.

**Epilogue**:
The Chapter "Après la guerre" (1945–1962), from the end of André Schlegel's tour of operations to his death.

**Carnet de vol**:
One of André Schlegel's personal flight logbooks, in which every flight and mission is recorded by hand. Each is a distinct book with its own title page; three are known from scans: the Carnet of 1938 (Tours, Sous-lieutenant), the Carnet of 1940 (Souk-el-Arba, Lieutenant) and the Carnet of 1947 (Villacoublay, Capitaine).
_Avoid_: carnet de bord, logbook, journal, "the carnet" as if there were only one

**Companion**:
The Carnet de vol that André Schlegel was filling at the current moment of the Narration. It opens the Journey, is present in every Scene, gives way to the next Carnet when he starts a new one, and closes the Journey.

**Spread**:
Two facing pages of a Carnet de vol, as scanned from the original.
_Avoid_: page, scan, sheet

**Reconstructed Spread**:
Two facing pages drawn for the site in the style of a Carnet de vol, for the months from October 1944 for which no original is scanned. It carries only facts attested by the Missions list or the Narration, leaves the rest blank, and is always marked as a reconstruction.
_Avoid_: facsimile, replica, fake page

**Missions list**:
The table of André Schlegel's sorties from Elvington (July 1944 – April 1945), giving for each its date, aircraft, target, incidents and citations.
_Avoid_: mission log, mission table

**Archive film**:
One of the four film sequences inside the Film: Bloch 210 bombing training, the DB-7 low-level raid, French crews at Lossiemouth, and the night mission over Germany.
_Avoid_: video, clip, footage

**Projection**:
The showing of an Archive film inside a Scene, as if thrown on a screen by a period projector, in step with the Narration.
_Avoid_: video player, embed
